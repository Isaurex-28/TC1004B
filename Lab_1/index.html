Keith Haring

Short Biography

Keith Haring was an american artist born in Pennsylvania, but based in New York City. His art was heavily inspired by graffiti culture One of his main gimmicks was portraying relevant social and political matters through a cartoonish, colorful and almost playful style His art also sought to portray challenges and daily occurences of LGBTQ+ people, being gay himself. After he was diagnosed with AIDS, he created the Keith Haring foundation, providing funding and imagery to people with AIDS.[cite: 2]

Visual style inspired by the artist

His art combined Pop Art with Street Art and culture, his art is unique for including pictograms, such as the basic man and dog. Keith's works inspired a simplicity in political art, sometimes less is able to transmir even more. He also put a contrast between the social causes and the childish cartoons.[cite: 2]
